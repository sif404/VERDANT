05 / research foundation
From Amra research to working cloud prototype.
VERDANT extends the conceptual foundation of Smart and Green Amra City. Historical temperature and humidity are NASA POWER T2M and RH2M; illuminance is estimated as irradiance × 109 lm/W × (1 − shade fraction). The reference point is not a surveyed stadium sensor location. No agronomic thresholds or irrigation recommendations are inferred.
